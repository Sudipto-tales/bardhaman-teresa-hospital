FAQ

We Are Here To Answer Your
Questions

You can easily book an appointment through our online portal by clicking the "Book Appointment" button at the top of the page, selecting your preferred doctor and time slot, and confirming your details.

We accept various payment methods including credit/debit cards, net banking, and UPI for online consultations. In-person visits can also be paid via cash or insurance.

Cabin bookings can be requested during admission or pre-booked by contacting our inpatient services desk at +011 3253 4567.

Insurance coverage depends on your specific policy and provider. Please contact our billing department with your policy details for a comprehensive breakdown.

General visiting hours are from 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM daily. ICU visiting hours are strictly between 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM and 5:00 PM - 6:00 PM.
