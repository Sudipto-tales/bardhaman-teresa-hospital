Medicine & Nephrology

Chronic care that fits around a working life

Dialysis is not a one-off event, it is a schedule someone lives with for years. We run early-morning, afternoon and evening shifts so treatment can sit around a job rather than replace it.

The general medicine side handles diabetes, thyroid, infection and the long tail of conditions that do not belong to any single organ specialty.

  • Three dialysis shifts daily
  • Isolation stations available
  • AV fistula creation on site
  • Transplant work-up and referral
  • Diabetes and thyroid clinics
  • Dietician attached to the unit

Regular slot guaranteed

Fixed weekly chair times for every long-term patient.

What We Do

Procedures & Services

Haemodialysis

Bicarbonate dialysis on reverse-osmosis water, with single-use dialysers on request.

Peritoneal Dialysis

Home-based CAPD, including catheter insertion and family training.

Kidney Biopsy

Ultrasound-guided biopsy with same-day histopathology dispatch.

AV Fistula Surgery

Access creation and salvage, planned so it matures before you need it.

Diabetes Clinic

HbA1c-led review, insulin titration and foot screening in one visit.

General Medicine OPD

Fever, infection, thyroid, anaemia — the everyday work of a physician.

Conditions the medicine team treats

Kidney disease rarely arrives alone. The physicians and nephrologists share a floor so a single visit covers both.
