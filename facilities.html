Our Facilities

Infrastructure that keeps the clock on your side

Most avoidable harm in a hospital comes from waiting — for a scan, a theatre, a bed. The building is laid out to remove those gaps: Emergency opens directly onto radiology, and radiology onto the theatre corridor.

A generator and water-treatment plant keep dialysis, ICU and theatre running through any outage, which in this district is not a theoretical concern.

  • Emergency next to radiology
  • Backup power for critical areas
  • Reverse-osmosis water plant
  • Central oxygen and suction
  • Lifts sized for trolleys
  • Accessible on every floor

Never off

Generator cover for ICU, theatre and dialysis.

What Is On Site

Twelve Services Under One Roof

24/7 Emergency

A resuscitation bay, triage nurse and duty physician on site every hour of the year.

Intensive Care

34 ICU beds across general, cardiac, neuro and neonatal units with one-to-one nursing.

Modular Operating Theatres

Four laminar-flow theatres, one kept free at all times for emergencies.

Laboratory

Pathology, microbiology and histopathology with same-day reporting on routine work.

Radiology & Imaging

Digital X-ray, ultrasound, colour Doppler and CT with consultant reporting.

Blood Bank Link

Licensed storage with a direct line to the district blood bank for rare groups.

24-Hour Pharmacy

In-house pharmacy stocking every drug on the hospital formulary, day and night.

Physiotherapy Unit

A gym-equipped department running inpatient rounds and outpatient courses.

Ambulance Fleet

Six vehicles, two of them advanced life support with a paramedic on board.

Accessible Throughout

Ramps, lifts and accessible toilets on every floor of the building.

Hospital Kitchen

Therapeutic diets cooked on site — renal, cardiac, diabetic and post-surgical.

Visitor Amenities

Free Wi-Fi, a cafeteria, prayer room and an attendant lounge on the ground floor.
