How to see one of them

Appointments open fourteen days ahead. Call, WhatsApp or use the form — all three reach the same desk and are confirmed the same day.

Book an appointment
  • Call +91 342 325 4567
  • WhatsApp the same number
  • Use the online form
  • Walk in for Emergency
  • Bring previous reports
  • Carry a photo ID
  • Insurance card if cashless
  • Arrive 15 minutes early
  • Reschedule free of charge
  • Follow-up within 14 days free
  • Second opinions welcome
  • Teleconsult on request

Find the right consultant

Tell us the symptom rather than the speciality — the desk will match you to the correct clinic.
